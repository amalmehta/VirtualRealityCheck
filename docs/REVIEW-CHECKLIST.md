# Manual Review Checklist

What the tests can't check.

**Does it do its job?**
- [ ] Take it for real. Did you do every lookup, or skip some?
- [ ] Did your biggest surprise tell you something you couldn't have predicted?
- [ ] Did you write a change you'd actually do?
- [ ] Do the lookup directions match your phone's current menus? (They were read from Apple's iOS 26 and Google's guides; labels for pickups and weekly averages weren't fully documented.)

**Feel and smoothness**
- [ ] Opening animation feels quick and doesn't repeat on navigation
- [ ] Typing and Next feel instant on a real phone
- [ ] Switching to Settings and back on an iPhone keeps your place, including after Safari reloads the tab
- [ ] Scrolling the result is fluid on an older phone
- [ ] Reduce Motion gives a plain fade

**Look**
- [ ] Every screen in light and dark, on phone and desktop
- [ ] The formula reads well in dark mode and on a phone, in Safari and Firefox
- [ ] Icon on the home screen and in a browser tab

**Privacy and security**
- [ ] The Network tab shows nothing leaving the site during a full run
- [ ] Delete everything really empties it (check after a reload)
- [ ] Opening the live site over HTTPS shows no mixed-content warning
- [ ] Feedback link attaches nothing from your answers

**Sources to read by hand**
- [ ] Parry et al. 2021: the "about 5% of studies" wording comes from the university's summary and the abstract, not the full text
- [ ] Ding et al. 2025 has a published erratum (Lancet Public Health 10(9):e731), not yet read
- [ ] Hunt 2018 and Allcott 2020 were checked from abstracts and summaries, not the full journal text
